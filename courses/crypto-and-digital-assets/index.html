Crypto and Digital Assets

A balanced, risk-focused introduction to cryptoassets: how blockchains, Bitcoin, Ethereum and stablecoins work, how wallets and exchanges can fail, why prices swing so far, how to spot scams, and what UK and US regulators and tax authorities say. Educational only — not a recommendation to buy.

Open this course in Veleta

What you’ll learn

  • Explain blockchains, Bitcoin, Ethereum, tokens and stablecoins
  • Compare custodial and self-custody wallets and assess counterparty risk
  • Quantify crypto volatility and recognise common scams
  • Describe UK and US regulation and tax at an educational level, and size risk sensibly

9 lessons in 3 units

Unit 1: How crypto works

  1. Money, ledgers and blockchains — What money does and how a shared ledger records it.
  2. Bitcoin basics — A fixed supply, proof of work and the halving.
  3. Ethereum, tokens and stablecoins — Smart contracts, the Merge and coins that try to hold a peg.

Unit 2: Holding crypto and its risks

  1. Wallets and keys — Who really controls your crypto — and how you can lose it.
  2. Exchanges and counterparty risk — What can go wrong when a firm holds your assets.
  3. Volatility and valuation — Deep drawdowns and why crypto is hard to value.

Unit 3: Staying safe

  1. Spotting crypto scams — Pump-and-dumps, rug pulls, fake giveaways and romance scams.
  2. Regulation and tax (educational overview) — What UK and US authorities say and how gains can be taxed.
  3. Position sizing and personal limits — Only invest what you can afford to lose.

Each unit ends with a checkpoint, and the course ends with a final exam (pass mark 80%). Passing earns a Veleta Finance course-completion certificate — an educational credential, not an accredited qualification.

Veleta Finance provides general financial education, not personalised financial advice. Investing involves risk, including possible loss of capital.

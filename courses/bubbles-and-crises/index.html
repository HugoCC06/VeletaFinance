Financial History: Bubbles and Crises

Explore four centuries of manias, panics and crashes — from Dutch tulips and the South Sea Bubble to 1929, the dot-com bust, the global financial crisis, COVID and the 2022 UK gilt crisis — and draw out the lessons that still apply to investors today.

Open this course in Veleta

What you’ll learn

  • Describe the classic stages of a bubble and recognise its warning signs
  • Explain the causes and consequences of major crashes from 1637 to 2022
  • Calculate falls from peak and the gains needed to recover
  • Draw lessons on leverage, herd behaviour, diversification and staying invested

9 lessons in 3 units

Unit 1: Anatomy of a bubble

  1. How bubbles form — The five classic stages, from new idea to panic.
  2. Tulip mania — The Dutch flower craze of the 1630s — and the debate about it.
  3. The South Sea and Mississippi bubbles — 1720: when two nations caught investing fever.

Unit 2: Crashes of the 20th century

  1. The 1929 crash and the Great Depression — From the roaring twenties to the deepest bear market.
  2. Black Monday 1987 — The biggest one-day percentage fall in Dow history.
  3. The dot-com bubble — When the internet was going to change everything — and did, eventually.

Unit 3: Modern crises and lessons

  1. The global financial crisis — Subprime mortgages, leverage and the fall of Lehman Brothers.
  2. Euro debt, COVID and the gilt crisis — Three very different shocks from the 2010s and 2020s.
  3. Lessons from history — What centuries of manias and crashes teach investors.

Each unit ends with a checkpoint, and the course ends with a final exam (pass mark 80%). Passing earns a Veleta Finance course-completion certificate — an educational credential, not an accredited qualification.

Understanding Stocks and Stock Markets

Learn what shares are, how exchanges in London and New York work, how orders get filled, what moves prices, and how currency affects your returns.

Open this course in Veleta

What you’ll learn

  • Explain what a share represents and why companies issue them
  • Interpret bid and ask prices, market capitalisation and order types
  • Describe major UK and US exchanges and indices
  • Explain why prices change and how currency affects international returns

11 lessons in 3 units

Unit 1: Owning a piece of a company

  1. What a share really is — Owning a slice of a real business.
  2. Why companies sell shares — Raising money, IPOs and the primary and secondary markets.
  3. How stock exchanges work — Where buyers meet sellers — in London, New York and beyond.

Unit 2: Prices and orders

  1. Share price and market capitalisation — Why a £2 share is not “cheaper” than a £200 share.
  2. Bid, ask and the spread — The two prices behind every quote.
  3. Market orders and limit orders — Choosing between speed and price control.
  4. Why prices move — and price versus value — Supply, demand, expectations and the idea of intrinsic value.

Unit 3: Market life

  1. Dividends and stock splits — Cash payouts, key dates and why splits don’t create value.
  2. Market indices — FTSE 100, S&P 500, Nasdaq and how indices summarise markets.
  3. Bull markets, bear markets and volatility — The language of market moods — and what volatility really means.
  4. UK and US markets, pounds and dollars — Pence quotes, stamp duty, settlement and currency exposure.

Each unit ends with a checkpoint, and the course ends with a final exam (pass mark 80%). Passing earns a Veleta Finance course-completion certificate — an educational credential, not an accredited qualification.

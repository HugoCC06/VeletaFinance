Property and Real Estate

Understand renting versus buying, deposits and loan-to-value, how mortgages work, how leverage magnifies gains and losses, and how buy-to-let, REITs and property funds compare — with UK and US examples.

Open this course in Veleta

What you’ll learn

  • Compare the total costs of renting and buying, including opportunity cost
  • Calculate LTV, mortgage interest, equity and the effect of leverage
  • Calculate gross and net rental yields and explain buy-to-let cash flow
  • Explain REITs, property fund liquidity, housing cycles and diversification

9 lessons in 3 units

Unit 1: Renting, buying and deposits

  1. Renting versus buying — Total costs, flexibility and the money your deposit could earn elsewhere.
  2. Deposits and loan-to-value — How much you put down shapes how much you borrow — and the rate you pay.
  3. Buying costs and affordability — One-off costs, income limits and stress tests.

Unit 2: Mortgages and leverage

  1. Types of mortgage — Repayment or interest-only, fixed or variable.
  2. How mortgage payments work — Amortisation, and why early payments are mostly interest.
  3. Equity, leverage and negative equity — How borrowing magnifies gains — and losses.

Unit 3: Investing in property

  1. Buy-to-let: yields and cash flow — Gross yield, net yield, voids and the costs of being a landlord.
  2. REITs and property funds — Owning property through the stock market — and the liquidity trap of open-ended funds.
  3. Cycles, interest rates and diversification — Why house prices move, and why your home is a concentrated bet.

Each unit ends with a checkpoint, and the course ends with a final exam (pass mark 80%). Passing earns a Veleta Finance course-completion certificate — an educational credential, not an accredited qualification.

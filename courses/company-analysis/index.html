Veleta Finance

Company Financial Analysis

Learn to read income statements, balance sheets and cash flow statements, calculate margins and returns, judge debt, and spot red flags — using practical exercises.

Open this course in Veleta

What you’ll learn

  • Interpret income statements, balance sheets and cash flow statements
  • Calculate and compare margins, ROE, ROIC, EPS and free cash flow
  • Assess leverage and financial resilience
  • Evaluate business quality and recognise accounting red flags

9 lessons in 3 units

Unit 1: How businesses make money

  1. What is a business model? — How a company creates value, and gets paid for it.
  2. The income statement — From revenue to net income, line by line.
  3. Margins and profitability — Turning profit into percentages you can compare.

Unit 2: Balance sheets, cash and debt

  1. The balance sheet — What a company owns, what it owes, and what is left for shareholders.
  2. The cash flow statement — Operating, investing and financing cash flows — and free cash flow.
  3. Debt and leverage — How borrowing magnifies both returns and risks.

Unit 3: Judging quality

  1. ROE, ROIC and EPS — How efficiently a company turns capital into profit.
  2. Growth, quality and competitive advantages — What makes a business durable — and why growth alone is not enough.
  3. Annual reports, limitations and red flags — Where to find the facts — and what the numbers can hide.

Each unit ends with a checkpoint, and the course ends with a final exam (pass mark 80%). Passing earns a Veleta Finance course-completion certificate — an educational credential, not an accredited qualification.

Veleta Finance provides general financial education, not personalised financial advice. Investing involves risk, including possible loss of capital.

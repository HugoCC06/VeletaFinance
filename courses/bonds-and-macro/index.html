Bonds, Interest Rates and Macroeconomics

Understand bonds, yields and credit risk, then explore inflation, GDP, central banks in the UK and US, the yield curve and how interest rates ripple through markets.

Open this course in Veleta

What you’ll learn

  • Explain bond terms, prices, yields, duration and credit risk
  • Describe GDP, inflation, unemployment and recessions
  • Compare the Bank of England and the Federal Reserve and their policy tools
  • Interpret the yield curve and the links between interest rates, currencies and share valuations

11 lessons in 3 units

Unit 1: Bonds

  1. What is a bond? — Lending money in exchange for interest and repayment.
  2. Bond prices and yields — Why bond prices fall when yields rise.
  3. Duration and interest-rate risk — How sensitive a bond is to changes in rates.
  4. Credit risk: government and corporate bonds — Will the borrower pay you back?

Unit 2: The economy and central banks

  1. GDP, growth and recessions — Measuring the size and health of an economy.
  2. Inflation and unemployment — Two numbers central banks watch closely.
  3. Central banks: the Bank of England and the Fed — Who sets interest rates, and why.
  4. Monetary versus fiscal policy — Central banks and governments pull different levers.

Unit 3: Markets and macro

  1. The yield curve — What borrowing costs across maturities can signal.
  2. Interest rates and share valuations — How the cost of money ripples through markets.
  3. Exchange rates — What moves the pound and the dollar.

Each unit ends with a checkpoint, and the course ends with a final exam (pass mark 80%). Passing earns a Veleta Finance course-completion certificate — an educational credential, not an accredited qualification.

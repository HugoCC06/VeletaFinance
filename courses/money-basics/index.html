Money Basics: Budgeting, Debt and Credit

Build the foundations every investor needs: a budget that works, an emergency fund, smart saving, borrowing without traps, a healthy credit record and a clear picture of student loans and payslips in the UK and US.

Open this course in Veleta

What you’ll learn

  • Build a budget using needs, wants and the 50/30/20 rule
  • Size an emergency fund and compare savings using AER and APY
  • Explain APR, common debt traps and debt payoff strategies
  • Describe credit scores, student loans and payslips in the UK and US at an educational level

9 lessons in 3 units

Unit 1: Budgeting and saving

  1. Building a simple budget — Needs, wants and the 50/30/20 rule.
  2. Emergency funds — A cash cushion for life’s surprises.
  3. Saving, interest and investing — AER, APY and when to save versus invest.

Unit 2: Borrowing and debt

  1. How borrowing costs work — APR, credit cards and the minimum payment trap.
  2. Good debt, bad debt and debt traps — Overdrafts, buy now pay later and payday loans.
  3. Paying off debt — The avalanche and snowball methods.

Unit 3: Credit, student loans and pay

  1. Credit scores and reports — What lenders see and how to build a good record.
  2. Student loans (educational overview) — How UK and US student loans differ.
  3. Reading your payslip — Gross pay, deductions and take-home pay.

Each unit ends with a checkpoint, and the course ends with a final exam (pass mark 80%). Passing earns a Veleta Finance course-completion certificate — an educational credential, not an accredited qualification.

Veleta Finance provides general financial education, not personalised financial advice. Investing involves risk, including possible loss of capital.

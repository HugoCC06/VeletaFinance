ETFs, Index Funds and Portfolio Construction

Understand funds, active versus passive investing, ETFs and trackers, then learn asset allocation, diversification, correlation, currency risk and rebalancing.

Open this course in Veleta

What you’ll learn

  • Explain how funds, ETFs and index trackers work and what they cost
  • Compare active and passive approaches using evidence
  • Construct an asset allocation and calculate weighted expected returns
  • Assess concentration, correlation and currency risk, and rebalance a portfolio

10 lessons in 3 units

Unit 1: Funds and trackers

  1. What is a fund? — Pooling money to buy many investments at once.
  2. Active versus passive investing — Trying to beat the market versus aiming to match it.
  3. ETFs, index funds and tracking — How trackers work, and how to judge how well they track.

Unit 2: Building a portfolio

  1. Asset allocation — The biggest decision in your portfolio.
  2. Geography, sectors and concentration — Hidden bets inside “diversified” portfolios.
  3. Correlation: why mixing assets works — Assets that don’t move together smooth the ride.
  4. Currency risk and hedging — What exchange rates do to an international portfolio.

Unit 3: Managing a portfolio

  1. Rebalancing — Keeping your mix on target as markets move.
  2. Portfolio volatility and time horizons — How your mix and your timeframe shape the range of outcomes.
  3. The limits of diversification — What spreading your money can — and can’t — protect you from.

Each unit ends with a checkpoint, and the course ends with a final exam (pass mark 80%). Passing earns a Veleta Finance course-completion certificate — an educational credential, not an accredited qualification.

Trading, Technical Analysis and Investor Psychology

Learn to read candlestick charts and common indicators, understand their limits, recognise the biases that trip investors up, and manage risk, leverage and position size responsibly.

Open this course in Veleta

What you’ll learn

  • Read OHLC and candlestick charts, moving averages and RSI, and explain their limitations
  • Contrast technical analysis, fundamental analysis and market efficiency
  • Recognise behavioural biases including overconfidence, loss aversion and FOMO
  • Apply position sizing and explain the risks of leverage, drawdowns and misleading backtests

11 lessons in 3 units

Unit 1: Charts and indicators

  1. Investing versus trading — Different time horizons, different games.
  2. Reading charts: OHLC, candlesticks and volume — The anatomy of a price chart.
  3. Trends, support and resistance — Lines traders draw — and why they are not laws of nature.
  4. Moving averages and RSI — Two popular indicators, calculated and critiqued.

Unit 2: Investor psychology

  1. Market efficiency and technical versus fundamental analysis — How much can you learn from prices alone?
  2. Overconfidence and confirmation bias — Why we think we know more than we do.
  3. Loss aversion and recency bias — Why losses sting and recent events loom large.
  4. Herd behaviour, FOMO and emotional decisions — When everyone is buying, should you?

Unit 3: Managing risk

  1. Risk management and position sizing — Deciding how much to risk on any one idea.
  2. Leverage and drawdowns — How borrowed money turns small moves into big losses.
  3. Why short-term trading is hard — and backtests mislead — Competition, costs, luck and overfitting.

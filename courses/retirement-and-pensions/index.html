Retirement and Pensions

Learn why retirement saving matters, how UK and US pensions and retirement accounts work, and how people invest for and draw an income through retirement. Educational only, not personal advice.

Open this course in Veleta

What you’ll learn

  • Explain how compounding and inflation shape long-term retirement planning
  • Describe the UK State Pension, workplace pensions and SIPPs at an educational level
  • Describe US Social Security, 401(k)s and traditional and Roth IRAs
  • Compare ways to invest for and draw an income in retirement, and the risks involved

9 lessons in 3 units

Unit 1: Why retirement saving matters

  1. Why plan for retirement? — Decades without a salary, and the jobs a pension has to do.
  2. Compounding over decades — Why the years you start matter more than you think.
  3. Inflation and your future income — Planning in what money can buy, not just in pounds or dollars.

Unit 2: Pensions in the UK and US

  1. State Pension and Social Security — How the UK and US state schemes work.
  2. UK workplace and personal pensions — Auto-enrolment, tax relief, DC versus DB, and SIPPs.
  3. US retirement accounts — 401(k)s, IRAs, employer matches and the Roth choice.

Unit 3: Investing through retirement

  1. Asset allocation through life — Glide paths, lifestyling and target-date funds.
  2. Turning a pot into an income — The 4% rule, annuities and drawdown.
  3. Sequence risk and the cost of fees — Why the order of returns and small charges matter so much.

Each unit ends with a checkpoint, and the course ends with a final exam (pass mark 80%). Passing earns a Veleta Finance course-completion certificate — an educational credential, not an accredited qualification.

Veleta Finance provides general financial education, not personalised financial advice. Investing involves risk, including possible loss of capital.

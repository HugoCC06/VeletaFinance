Valuation and Company Analysis

From P/E ratios to discounted cash flows: learn the core valuation tools, how to use them sensibly, and why every valuation is an estimate built on assumptions.

Open this course in Veleta

What you’ll learn

  • Calculate and interpret P/E, P/S, P/B, EV/EBITDA, dividend and FCF yields
  • Apply present value and simple discounted cash flow models
  • Use comparable companies and match methods to business types
  • Explain valuation sensitivity, margin of safety, value traps and justified premiums

12 lessons in 3 units

Unit 1: Multiples

  1. Price, value and expectations — Why valuation is about the future, not the past.
  2. The price-to-earnings ratio — The most quoted valuation multiple — and its traps.
  3. Price-to-sales and price-to-book — Multiples for when earnings don’t tell the whole story.
  4. Enterprise value and EV/EBITDA — Valuing the whole business, debt included.

Unit 2: Cash flow valuation

  1. Present value: money now versus later — Why £100 today is worth more than £100 next year.
  2. Discount rates and required returns — Choosing the rate that turns future cash into value today.
  3. Discounted cash flow (DCF) fundamentals — Adding up a business’s future cash, in today’s money.
  4. Sensitivity and margin of safety — Why valuation outputs are ranges, not answers.

Unit 3: Valuation in practice

  1. Dividend yield and free cash flow yield — Measuring cash returned — or available — per pound invested.
  2. Comparable company analysis — Valuing a business by looking at its peers.
  3. Growth, mature and cyclical businesses — Why different businesses need different valuation approaches.
  4. Value traps and justified premiums — When cheap is expensive, and expensive is fair.

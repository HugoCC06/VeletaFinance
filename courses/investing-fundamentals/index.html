Investing Fundamentals

Start here. Learn what investing is, how risk and return are linked, why compounding and inflation matter, and how to avoid the most common mistakes.

Open this course in Veleta

What you’ll learn

  • Explain the purpose, risks and basic mechanics of investing
  • Distinguish saving, investing and speculation
  • Calculate compound growth and real (inflation-adjusted) returns
  • Explain diversification, liquidity, time horizons and the impact of costs

11 lessons in 3 units

Unit 1: Why invest?

  1. What is investing? — Putting money to work today in the hope of having more tomorrow.
  2. Saving versus investing — When cash is the right home for your money — and when it is not.
  3. Inflation and purchasing power — Why £100 today will not buy what £100 buys in ten years.
  4. The power of compounding — Earning returns on your returns — and why time matters so much.

Unit 2: Risk and return

  1. Risk and return — Why higher potential returns come with a bumpier ride.
  2. Time horizons and liquidity — How long you can wait — and how quickly you can get your money out.
  3. Don’t put all your eggs in one basket — Diversification: spreading risk so one bad outcome does not sink you.
  4. Investing versus speculation — Owning productive assets versus betting on price moves.

Unit 3: The investor’s toolkit

  1. The main asset classes — Cash, bonds, shares, property and commodities at a glance.
  2. The hidden power of costs — Small fees compound into big differences.
  3. Common investing myths — Clearing up five misconceptions that stop people investing well.

Each unit ends with a checkpoint, and the course ends with a final exam (pass mark 80%). Passing earns a Veleta Finance course-completion certificate — an educational credential, not an accredited qualification.
